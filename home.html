What it does

Finds bookings automatically

Connect Gmail and Landed reads your confirmation emails — flights, hotels, cars, trains — and adds them for your review. Read-only access; it never sends, deletes, or modifies anything in your inbox.

Organizes by trip

Bookings group into trips automatically, so a week in Tokyo or a weekend drive shows every reservation together, in order.

Tracks your budget

Set a budget per trip and see spend broken down by category — flights, lodging, food, activities — as bookings and expenses come in.

Works when you're offline

Your bookings, itinerary, and trip photos stay available even without a signal, so you're never stuck without your confirmation number at the gate.

How it works

1

Connect Gmail

2

3

Trips, dates, confirmation numbers, and budgets — organized automatically, ready before you travel.
