# Landed Support

Need help with Landed? We're happy to assist.

## Contact us

Email **landedconcierge@gmail.com** and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

## What we can help with

- Trouble signing in or connecting your Gmail account
- A booking that didn't sync correctly, or one that's missing details
- Questions about how Landed works or what data it collects (see our [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy.html))
- Deleting your account or your data
- Bug reports and feature requests

## Before you write in

A couple of quick checks that solve most issues:

- Make sure you're on the latest version of Landed from the App Store.
- If a booking looks off, try pulling down to refresh the screen — Landed syncs from Gmail periodically, not instantly.
- If you're offline, some screens show saved data from your last sync rather than live data — that's expected, and everything catches up once you're back online.

Still stuck? Email us at landedconcierge@gmail.com with a short description of what happened, and we'll take it from there.
